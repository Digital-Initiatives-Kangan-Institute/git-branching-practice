Where do they live?

Cockroaches occupy a wide variety of habitats. Wild species can be found in forests, caves, grasslands, beneath bark, and among decomposing vegetation. Pest species may seek food, water, warmth, and shelter inside buildings.

If they are indoors

Keep food sealed, clean crumbs and spills, empty rubbish regularly, fix water leaks, and seal gaps around pipes and walls. Persistent infestations are best handled with targeted pest-management methods rather than indiscriminate spraying.
