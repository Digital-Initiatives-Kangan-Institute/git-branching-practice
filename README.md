# Classic Vanilla Cake

A moist, tender, and buttery classic vanilla layer cake paired with rich vanilla buttercream frosting.

## Overview
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Bake Time:** 25–30 minutes
- **Total Time:** 50 minutes (plus cooling)
- **Yield:** 1 two-layer 8-inch (20 cm) cake (12 servings)

---

## Ingredients

### Vanilla Cake
- **2 ½ cups (315 g)** all-purpose flour (plain flour)
- **2 ½ tsp** baking powder
- **½ tsp** fine sea salt
- **¾ cup (170 g)** unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
- **1 ¾ cups (350 g)** granulated white sugar
- **4 large** eggs, at room temperature
- **1 tbsp** pure vanilla extract
- **1 cup (240 ml)** whole milk, at room temperature

### Vanilla Buttercream Frosting
- **1 cup (225 g)** unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
- **4 cups (480 g)** powdered sugar (icing sugar), sifted
- **2–3 tbsp (30–45 ml)** heavy cream or whole milk
- **2 tsp** pure vanilla extract
- **Pinch** of fine sea salt

---

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Pans & Oven
1. Preheat your oven to **175°C (350°F)**.
2. Grease two **8-inch (20 cm)** round cake pans with butter or non-stick spray, line the bottoms with parchment paper, and lightly flour the sides.

### 2. Mix the Dry Ingredients
1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the **flour**, **baking powder**, and **salt**. Set aside.

### 3. Cream Butter and Sugar
1. In a large bowl using a stand mixer or hand mixer on medium-high speed, beat the **softened butter** and **granulated sugar** together for **3–4 minutes** until pale, light, and fluffy. Scrape down the sides of the bowl as needed.

### 4. Add Eggs and Vanilla
1. Add the **eggs** one at a time, beating well on medium speed after each addition until fully incorporated.
2. Mix in the **vanilla extract**.

### 5. Combine Wet and Dry Ingredients
1. Reduce the mixer speed to low. Add the dry flour mixture in **three additions**, alternating with the **milk** in **two additions** (begin and end with the flour mixture).
2. Mix only until just combined—avoid overmixing to keep the crumb soft and tender.

### 6. Bake
1. Divide the batter evenly between the two prepared pans and smooth the tops with a spatula.
2. Bake for **25–30 minutes**, or until the tops are golden and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs.
3. Cool the cakes in the pans on a wire rack for **10 minutes**, then turn them out onto the rack to cool completely before frosting.

### 7. Make the Frosting & Assemble
1. Beat the **softened butter** on medium speed for **2 minutes** until smooth and creamy.
2. Gradually add the **sifted powdered sugar** on low speed, then add **2 tablespoons cream**, **vanilla extract**, and **salt**.
3. Increase speed to medium-high and beat for **3 minutes** until light and fluffy (add the remaining tablespoon of cream if needed for spreadability).
4. Place one cooled cake layer on a serving plate, spread an even layer of frosting on top, add the second layer, and frost the top and sides.
